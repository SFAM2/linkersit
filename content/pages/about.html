About LinkersIT

Different perspectives.
One shared ambition.

Technology consulting and hands-on delivery for consulting firms, enterprise teams and ambitious founders.

Good technology
starts with
understanding people.

LinkersIT is a technology consulting and delivery partner with a team positioned in Paris and Tunis. We work with consulting firms seeking technical support, enterprise teams developing digital solutions and startups building their products.

Our role is to connect business objectives with technical execution. We help you evaluate the options, define a realistic scope and bring together the design and engineering work needed to deliver it.

For consulting teams, that can mean supporting a client workstream. For an enterprise, it can mean improving an existing application. For a founder, it can mean shaping and building a first product. The engagement starts with your needs.
