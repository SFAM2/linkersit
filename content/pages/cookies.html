Cookies & browser storage

A clear view of what this website stores in your browser.

Current website behavior · Updated 5 October 2026

No optional trackers

This website has no analytics or advertising tools. Fonts and animations are hosted with the site. Contact verification is provided by Google reCAPTCHA and loads only after you select “Enable verification”. Google may use cookies and device information for that service; see its Privacy Policy.

The notice preference

The notice preference stored by the website is linkersit-cookie-notice-v1. When you click “Got it”, its value becomes dismissed. This is local storage, not a cookie; it is not sent with page requests.

Purpose: remember that you dismissed the information notice. Contents: the word “dismissed”. Duration: until you reset the preference or clear site storage. No name, email address or project information is stored in it.

Your controls

The contact form sets a linkersit_form cookie when verification is enabled. It protects the submission and expires after 30 minutes. It is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite Strict. Google manages any cookies used by reCAPTCHA; clearing this website’s notice preference does not clear Google cookies. Use your browser settings to manage those.

Use “Cookie information” in the footer to view this information and reset the notice preference. You can also clear site data using your browser settings. The site remains usable if storage is blocked.

There are no analytics or advertising settings to enable because those tools are not installed.

Hosting and future changes

The hosting service may process technical request information to deliver and secure the site; see the privacy notice. This page describes the current website code, not unrelated cookies used when you visit external sites.

If optional trackers are added later, this information and the relevant controls must be updated before those tools run. See CNIL information on cookies and trackers.
