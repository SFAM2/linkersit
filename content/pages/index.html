A few useful answers

Before we
get started.

How do you support consulting firms?

We can contribute technology consulting, product design and engineering to your client engagements. We agree on the scope, working arrangements, confidentiality needs and responsibilities before the work begins.

Do I need a detailed brief?

No. Start with the problem you want to solve, who you want to reach and any constraints you already know. We can use that conversation to define a useful brief together.

How are pricing and timelines decided?

They depend on scope, complexity and the content or integrations involved. We discuss your needs before preparing a proposal. Contacting us does not commit you to a project.

How do you help startups?

We help founders clarify the problem, prioritize an MVP, prototype the experience and build a first release. For an existing product, we can review usability and technical needs, then plan the next iteration around your stage and budget.

How do I contact the team?

Email contact@linkersit.com or use the project enquiry form. Complete the spam verification and send your enquiry directly to the team. If the online form is unavailable, email us directly.
